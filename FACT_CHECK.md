# Public-Facing Fact Check and Positioning Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-09-06

## Verified ISO facts

The following statements are supported by ISO's current public materials:

- The reference is **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**.
- Its official title is **Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system**.
- It specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).
- It is intended for organizations that develop, provide, or use AI systems and is applicable across industries and organization sizes.
- ISO describes ISO/IEC 42001 as a management system standard using the Plan-Do-Check-Act approach.
- Certification is voluntary. ISO itself does not certify organizations; certification is performed by independent certification bodies.
- ISO/IEC 42001 does not replace applicable laws or regulations.

## What is original to this repository

This repository is **not** a clause-by-clause reproduction of ISO/IEC 42001. The following are original implementation examples created for this portfolio:

- repository folder structure;
- eight-question governance checklist;
- risk tiers and risk-scoring method;
- example approval workflow;
- role titles and RACI assignments;
- control identifiers and control matrix;
- suggested review frequencies;
- templates, checklists, registers, and evidence examples;
- fictional loan-underwriting case study and its risk classification.

These examples are intended to demonstrate how an organization might operationalize AI governance. They are not presented as verbatim ISO requirements, a certification checklist, legal advice, or a guarantee of conformity.
