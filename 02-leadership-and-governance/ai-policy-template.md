# AI Policy Template

## Policy statement
The organization will develop, acquire, deploy, and use AI systems in a manner that is governed, risk-based, secure, transparent where appropriate, subject to human accountability, and monitored throughout the lifecycle.

## Principles
- Accountability remains with named human and organizational owners.
- AI risks and impacts are assessed before material deployment.
- Data is governed for quality, provenance, privacy, security, and appropriateness.
- Higher-impact use cases receive stronger validation and oversight.
- Human review is retained when automated outcomes could materially affect individuals or organizations.
- AI systems are monitored after deployment.
- Incidents and material changes trigger reassessment.

## Applicability
Applies to employees, contractors, vendors, and business units involved in AI development, procurement, operation, or use.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented risk acceptance by an authorized accountable owner.

## Review
Review at least annually or when there is a material regulatory, technology, business, or risk change.
