# AI Governance Committee Charter

## Mandate
Provide oversight for material AI use cases and ensure risks, impacts, controls, ownership, and monitoring are appropriate before and after deployment.

## Responsibilities
- Approve high-impact AI use cases.
- Review risk and impact assessments.
- Challenge human-oversight design.
- Review material incidents and control failures.
- Review significant model, data, vendor, or purpose changes.
- Escalate unacceptable residual risk.

## Suggested membership
Business, Technology, AI/Data, Security, Privacy, Legal/Compliance, Risk, Model Validation, Operations.

## Decision outcomes
- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Deferred pending remediation
- Rejected

## Minimum evidence
- System description
- Business purpose
- Data assessment
- Impact assessment
- Risk assessment
- Validation evidence
- Human-oversight design
- Monitoring plan
- Named accountable owner
