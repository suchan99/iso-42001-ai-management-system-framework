# Loan Underwriting AI — Monitoring Plan

| Metric | Frequency | Warning trigger | Breach response | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predictive performance | Monthly | Material deterioration vs baseline | Validation review | Model owner |
| Data drift | Weekly | Feature distribution exceeds threshold | Investigate source / recalibrate | Data owner |
| Fairness metrics | Monthly | Material group disparity above approved tolerance | Escalate and restrict use if necessary | Model Risk |
| Human override rate | Monthly | Sudden material change | Review reviewer behavior and model quality | Operations |
| Complaints / appeals | Monthly | Upward trend or material event | Governance review | Business owner |
| Critical AI incidents | Event-driven | Any critical event | Immediate containment and escalation | AIMS owner |
