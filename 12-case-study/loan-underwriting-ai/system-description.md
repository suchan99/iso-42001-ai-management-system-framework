# Case Study: AI-Assisted Loan Underwriting

## Scenario
A fictional retail lender introduces an AI model that produces a credit-risk recommendation for consumer loan applications. The AI does not independently issue the final adverse decision; trained underwriters review designated outcomes.

## Intended purpose
For this fictional example, support underwriting by estimating credit risk from application and credit-history variables that the organization has determined are lawful, appropriate, documented, and governed for the intended use.

## Out-of-scope uses
- Marketing segmentation
- Employment decisions
- Insurance decisions
- Any use of unapproved sensitive attributes

## Affected parties
Loan applicants are directly affected. Underwriters, customer-service teams, compliance teams, and business management are operationally affected.

## Material governance concerns
- Historical bias in training data
- Proxy discrimination
- Explainability of adverse outcomes
- Automation bias by underwriters
- Data quality
- Model drift
- Incorrect adverse decisions
- Over-reliance on vendor or external credit data
