# AIMS Management Review

## Inputs
- Status of prior actions
- Changes in internal/external context
- AI objectives and KPIs
- Audit results
- Incidents and complaints
- Risk trends
- Monitoring results
- Vendor issues
- Resource / competence needs
- Regulatory developments
- Improvement opportunities

## Decisions / outputs
- Policy changes
- Risk-treatment priorities
- Resource changes
- Control improvements
- Systems requiring reassessment
- Actions, owners, and due dates
