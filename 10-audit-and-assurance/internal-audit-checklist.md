# AIMS Internal Audit Checklist

## Governance
- [ ] AIMS scope is current
- [ ] AI policy is approved and communicated
- [ ] Roles and authorities are assigned
- [ ] AI objectives are measured

## Inventory and risk
- [ ] AI inventory is complete
- [ ] Material use cases have current impact assessments
- [ ] Material use cases have current risk assessments
- [ ] Residual risks are accepted by authorized owners

## Lifecycle
- [ ] Design and deployment evidence exists
- [ ] Material changes trigger reassessment
- [ ] Human oversight operates as designed
- [ ] Monitoring is active

## Third parties
- [ ] Critical AI vendors are assessed
- [ ] Contract controls address relevant AI risks

## Assurance
- [ ] Incidents are tracked
- [ ] Corrective actions are closed with evidence
- [ ] Management review occurs on schedule
- [ ] Continual-improvement actions are documented
